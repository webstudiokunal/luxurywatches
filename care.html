Care for the long run

Keep your watch
in its rhythm.

A few thoughtful habits help preserve the finish, feel, and daily pleasure of your watch.

A considered routine

Care is part of the craft.

These are simple general suggestions. For model-specific guidance, including water resistance and servicing, consult the documentation supplied with your watch or contact the maison.

01 / DAILY WEAR
◌

Wipe with care.

After wearing, gently wipe the case and bracelet with a clean, soft cloth to remove fingerprints and everyday residue.

02 / STORAGE
⌂

Give it a safe place.

When not being worn, keep your watch in its presentation box or another clean, dry place away from knocks and scratches.

03 / ENVIRONMENT
◈

Avoid extremes.

Protect the watch from strong impacts, prolonged heat, and sudden temperature changes. Keep it away from powerful magnets.

04 / WATER
≈

Check before exposure.

Water resistance can vary by model and condition. Follow the specific instructions for your watch and never operate the crown when wet.

Good to know

Care, in a few words.

How should I clean the watch?

Use a soft, dry cloth for routine care. Before using water or any cleaning product, check the instructions for your exact model.

Where can I ask about servicing?

Contact the maison with your model details and we will guide you on the appropriate service route.

What if I no longer have the watch documentation?

Reach out to our team with any information you have about your watch so we can help identify the relevant guidance.
